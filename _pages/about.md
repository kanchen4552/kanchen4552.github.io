Kan Chen is a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Population Health Sciences at Weill Cornell Medicine, Cornell University.

He is currently a postdoctoral research fellow jointly mentored by [Prof. Xihong Lin](https://hsph.harvard.edu/research/lin-lab/) at the Biostatistics Department of Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health and [Prof. Zhonghua Liu](https://www.publichealth.columbia.edu/profile/zhonghua-liu-scd) at the Biostatistics Department of Columbia University since 2024.

Prior to this, Kan received his PhD in Applied Mathematics and Computational Science from the University of Pennsylvania in 2023 and dual MA degrees in Statistics and Data Science from the Wharton School in 2021, jointly mentored by [Prof. Dylan Small](https://statistics.wharton.upenn.edu/profile/dsmall/) and [Prof. Qi Long](https://www.med.upenn.edu/apps/faculty/index.php/g275/p8939931).

His research spans causal inference, causal machine learning and AI, high-dimensional statistics, and large-scale biomedical data analysis, with applications in clinical trials, oncology studies, genomics, public health, and policy evaluation.
